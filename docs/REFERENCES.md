# Algorithm references

These references credit the mathematical methods. The source files link back
here so a reader can distinguish an established method from a package-specific
policy or diagnostic. Code and data provenance are recorded separately in
[Release provenance](RELEASE_PROVENANCE.md) and [MIGRATION.md](../MIGRATION.md).

| Implementation | Reference and scope |
| --- | --- |
| `src/atmospheric_parameters/modal_variances.jl` | Winker (1991), [Effect of a finite outer scale on the Zernike decomposition of atmospheric optical turbulence](https://opg.optica.org/josaa/abstract.cfm?uri=josaa-8-10-1568), motivates variance-based outer-scale estimation. AOC profiles arbitrary supplied modal templates with an explicit discrepancy tolerance. |
| `src/docrime/{covariance,interaction_matrix}.jl` | Lai et al. (2021), [DO-CRIME](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/501/3/3443/6043240). The incremental centered sums use the recurrence of Welford (1962), [Corrected sums of squares and products](https://doi.org/10.1080/00401706.1962.10490022), generalized to cross-products. |
| `src/misregistration/meta_sensitivity_estimate.jl` | Héritier et al. (2021), [SPRINT](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stab1177). Lai et al. (2024), [ESCAPE](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3020489), combine DO-CRIME measurements and SPRINT registration before generating a pseudo-synthetic matrix. ESCAPE acquisition and instrument orchestration live in the instrument repository. |
| `src/optical_gains/gain_sensing.jl` | Chambouleyron et al. (2020), [Pyramid optical gains and the convolutional model](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202037836), and Chambouleyron et al. (2021), [Focal-plane-assisted pyramid wavefront sensor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.02297). |
| `src/optical_gains/{modal_dither_response,bounded_update}.jl` | Héritier et al. (2021), [SPRINT](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stab1177), provides context for temporal probes. AOC uses a harmonic least-squares fit and its own validity and bounded-update policy. |
| `src/modal_gain_optimization/modal_gain_optimization.jl` | Gendron and Léna (1994), *Astronomical adaptive optics. I. Modal control optimization*, A&A 291, 337–347. Welch (1967), [Averaging modified periodograms](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAU.1967.1161901), supplies the PSD method. AOC evaluates a discrete gain grid against a supplied delay and stability margin. |
| `src/phase_retrieval/{solver,device_solver,types}.jl` | Meimon, Fusco and Mugnier (2010), [LIFT](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.35.003036). AOC owns estimator state and the numerical solve with a caller-supplied forward model. |
| `src/focal_plane_sharpening/spsa.jl` | Spall (1992), [Multivariate stochastic approximation using a simultaneous perturbation gradient approximation](https://doi.org/10.1109/9.119632). |
| `src/focal_plane_sharpening/adam.jl` | Kingma and Ba (2015), [Adam](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.6980). |
| `src/focal_plane_sharpening/{modal_scan,weighted_core_intensity}.jl` | The weighted-core objective and quadratic scan policy follow the SPIDERS experiment described in [the sharpening design](DESIGN_SHARPENING.md). These are package-specific policies. |
| `src/probe_bases/{push_pull,spatial_sinusoidal}.jl` and `src/interaction_matrices/{central_difference,structured}.jl` | Conventional two-sided finite differences and Fourier basis functions. The AO precedent for multiplexed Hadamard probes is Meimon, Petit and Fusco (2015), [Optimized calibration with slope-oriented Hadamard actuation](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.23.027134). The AOC signed-code design and shape contracts are described in their methods. |
| `src/shack_hartmann/{subaperture_threshold,weighted_centroid_map}.jl` | Thomas et al. (2006), [Comparison of centroid computation algorithms in a Shack–Hartmann sensor](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.10661.x), reviews thresholded and weighted centroids. The threshold statistic and weighting policy are specific to this package. |
| `src/reconstructors/{svd_inverse,modal,weighted}.jl` | Penrose (1955), [A generalized inverse for matrices](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100030401), for the pseudoinverse. Tikhonov (1963), *Solution of incorrectly formulated problems and the regularization method*, for regularization. Ellerbroek (1994), [First-order performance evaluation of adaptive-optics systems](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.11.000783), for AO covariance-weighted reconstruction. The two-poke check, nulling and stroke-weighted interfaces are package contracts. |
| `src/tomography/{covariance_reconstructor,von_karman_covariance}.jl` | Ellerbroek (1994), [Minimum-variance wavefront reconstruction](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.11.000783), and Tokovinin (2002), *From Differential Image Motion to Seeing*, [finite outer-scale structure function](https://doi.org/10.1086/342683). The device Bessel approximation was adapted from the source identified in [Release provenance](RELEASE_PROVENANCE.md). |
| `src/modal_bases/{influence_function_eigenbasis,karhunen_loeve_basis,modal_fitting,modal_opd_expansion}.jl` | Dai (1995), [Modal compensation with Zernike polynomials and Karhunen–Loève functions](https://opg.optica.org/josaa/abstract.cfm?uri=josaa-12-10-2182), for the AO Karhunen–Loève and outer-scale context. The influence Gram eigenbasis, regularized coordinate fitting, and linear expansion implement the stated linear algebra in instrument-supplied coordinates. |
| `src/reference_frames/{dark_frame_moments,lamp_reference,pyramid_reference}.jl` | Welford (1962), [online mean and variance](https://doi.org/10.1080/00401706.1962.10490022), for the dark-frame recurrence. The lamp and pyramid qualification and validity rules are instrument-derived policies whose source revisions are recorded in the migration ledger. |
| `src/diagnostics/{held_out,interaction_matrix_stability,repeated_response_moments}.jl` | Package-specific validation metrics and policies for residuals, repeatability and stability. The centered online response moments use Welford (1962), [Corrected sums of squares and products](https://doi.org/10.1080/00401706.1962.10490022). |

The accelerator extensions implement the same scientific contracts as their
corresponding CPU sources. The [calibration catalogue](CALIBRATION_CATALOGUE.md)
records additional software references and what was compared with each project.
